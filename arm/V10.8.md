# lymb v10.8: no perforations, aluminium drums, two colourways, neck fixed

Cam asked for this pass (2026-10-21): no dot-grids anywhere on the visible arm, a minimal reBot-like look in rose's
language, and no brown. Design only; nothing is ordered. `LYMB_V108=0` rebuilds v10.7, and v9 is untouched.

## 1. The neck: what was wrong

`humanoid_export.py` (v10.2 to v10.7) dropped every `cover-head*` and `head-*` part along with the old cube head.
That removed quad00's pass-2/3 saddle cheeks (`cover-headcheek`), the collar (`cover-dockcollar`), `head-H1` and the
dock, and left them unpitched, so the cortex sat on a bare plate over the silver pitch drum.

Now:
- Only the old helmet, visor, eye and ear parts are dropped, and quad00's own `vcad/full/full.loon` is the list of
  what the body wears.
- Every part on the head-pitch body is rotated with the head. The exporter's sign was also flipped (-35 deg in
  trimesh's convention), so the dock sits square under the cortex.
- Built from origin/main 6bc527b (cortex-print merged). `vcad_export.py full` lists cover-necksleeve,
  cover-dockcollar, cover-headcheek, cover-headhub, trim-neckring and trim-neckpuck_l/r. None of them is dropped.
- `lymb-v10.8-*-neck-closeup.png` is the render to set against quad00's `renders/neck/pass3-neck-closeup-pitch35.png`.

## 2. Cooling without perforations (thermal_v10.py, the v10.8 default)

| joint | drum / path | derate | continuous |
|---|---|---|---|
| J2 (RS04) | full 6061 drum: 1.0 mm barrel and 1.2 mm solid caps, black anodized, on a 0.25 mm gap pad round the can | 0 % (model 1.12 x bare, capped at 1.0) | hold **1.33 kg** straight out, desk and humanoid, after the added mass |
| J3 (RS02) | 6061 band under the sleeve (below), plates through the sleeve | 21 % | **3.46 N·m**, at or above the 3.43 N·m cap (MARGIN 0.78 unchanged) |
| J4 (RS04) | 6061 caps on the motor faces (pad), sealed black PA12 barrel, Al flank panels kept | 11 % | hold 4.32 kg |
| J6 (RS02) | 6061 caps on the motor faces (pad), sealed PA12 barrel, ends closed (honest) | 16 % | hold 1.30 kg |
| J7 (RS00) | sealed sleeve, no band grid | 43 % | open: see below |

Notes:
- **The no-compensation case (solid caps, grids gone): J2 0.565 kg.** That was reported before compensating, as Cam
  asked.
- **J2 hold against the drum choice:**

  | drum choice | J2 hold |
  |---|---|
  | full drums at all three joints | 1.22 to 1.26 kg |
  | J4 and J6 with caps only (chosen) | 1.33 kg |

  J4 and J6 have thermal margin to spare, so they carry only the 6061 caps.
- **J3:** the band's wrap fraction barely matters. The bottleneck is the plates-to-air path. Putting Al panels over
  the J3 end plates (`LYMB_J3PANEL=1`, the same as J2/J4) raises J3 to 3.81 N·m if more margin is wanted.
- **J7:** with no grid the RS00 is skin-limited. Its continuous load is small (a tool roll), but the number should be
  checked on the rig before a heavy tool lives there.
- Every number is a lumped model with assumed h. It needs the elbow rig's heat test.

## 5. Look and colour (rose's language)

- **Caps:** a flush solid disc recessed 0.5 mm inside a 1.5 mm gunmetal ring. No graphite and no dots. "Rings are the
  joints, caps are quiet."
- **One accent:** a 2 mm Make-green inlay ring at the tool flange. Links are plain: no stripes, logos or panels.
- **Colourways:** `LYMB_CW=black|white`.
  - **all-black:** links #121213, drums black anodize.
  - **white with black joints:** links #ECECE9; joints, drums and caps black.
  - The humanoid covers and cortex shell follow the link tone, with a black or clear floor plate.
  - Gunmetal (0.30, 0.32, 0.34) stays at the rings on both. No brown anywhere.
