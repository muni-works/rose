The outer face's field stops 15° of the corner round + 3 mm short of the front edge. Holes are 2.5 mm deep into the dark sleeve (dark on dark). Left and right arms mirror (`ROSE_ARM_SIDE`). |
| upper arm visibly heavier than the forearm | biceps 122 x 140 against a forearm of 84 x 57 |
| J2 > J4 > J6 rings | the rings match the sleeve's width where it meets each joint: J2 Ø137 x 122, J4 Ø126 x 90, J6 Ø96 x 84 |

**Not met: the deltoid cap overlap.** On the humanoid, J2's axis runs fore-aft and the upper arm hangs below the J2 ring, so the sleeve meets the ring, not the cap. A cap that overlapped the sleeve by 8-10 mm would have to wrap the ring's lower half too. That would cover the rest of J2's dot-grid (J2 derate 3 % → about 10 %, J2 hold below 1.27 kg) and stop the J2 swing near 0°. I kept the hood to the top third. Rose should decide whether "cap over sleeve" can read as "cap over ring, ring over sleeve".

## 3. Thermal

- **J3:**
  - The grid is real holes. Open area is 2231 mm² (Ø1.4 / 2.2, three fields) against 2830 mm² for the v10.3 four-face Ø1.3 / 2.3 grid.
  - Derate **21 %**. Continuous **3.49 N·m**, above the **3.43 N·m** pose cap; the cap is unchanged.
  - The Ø1.3 / 2.3 grid in the same fields would not hold the cap (derate 22 %, continuous ~3.43 N·m with no margin).
- **J2 / J4:** open faces unchanged. J2 1 % desk, 3 % humanoid (the hood); J4 1 %.
- **Holds, model jaws:** J2 **1.369 kg desk / 1.299 kg humanoid**; J4 4.98 kg; J6 1.49 kg. Sag 0.45 mm. Mass 8.58 kg desk.

## 4. What the fuller biceps costs: range

The biceps is 122 deep x 140 wide, held to 55 mm on the torso side (arm -z) so it clears the turret shell below J2. That fuller section shortens the skinned single-joint ranges (`skin_clash.ranges`), and the soft limits now carry them:

| joint | v10.4 | v10.5 |
|---|---|---|
| J2, desk | -140 .. +10 | **-95 .. +5** |
| J2, humanoid (hood) | -75 .. +10 | -75 .. **+5** |
| J4 | ±110 | **±100** |
| J6 | ±100 | ±100 |

If rose wants J2 / J4 back, the biceps' fore-aft depth (a) is the lever, not its width.

Soft limits re-run on main's torso (table rebuilt; every kept pose re-checked against the real meshes and the bare structure):

| sweep | poses | kept | kept but clashing |
|---|---|---|---|
| desk, random inside the structure's ranges | 500 | 131 (26 %) | **0** |
| desk, fold grid J2 x J4 x J6 over the skinned ranges | 1372 | 1263 (92 %) | **0** |
| body_l, random | 300 | 64 (21 %) | **0** |
| body_r, random | 300 | 63 (21 %) | **0** |

The kept fraction of the whole structural range dropped from 45-48 % (v10.4) to 21-26 %, almost all from the per-joint ranges above.
