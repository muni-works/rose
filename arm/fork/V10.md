# lymb v10: one arm for the desk and the H2-class humanoid

`ROSE_ARM=v10` in `rose_fork.py`. With ROSE_ARM unset, v9 is bit-identical: the same `build()`, `world_loon()`, audit output and `fab/` package, checked by diff.

## What changed and why

1. **The roll moved from the forearm (J5) to the upper arm (J3).** The order is now J1 yaw (RS04), J2 pitch (RS04), J3 upper-arm roll (RS02), J4 elbow (RS04), J6 wrist pitch (RS02), J7 tool roll (RS00). It uses the same six motors at the same cost.
   - J3 is a cartridge just past the shoulder. The J2 holder plates end at an end plate at x = 105 mm, and the RS02 back-mounts on its far face.
   - A bridge on the J3 output carries the elbow holder plates (new link `upperd`). Two tabbed covers sit between those plates for torsion.
   - The forearm is the v9 `NO_J5` forearm: J6's holders tab straight into the forearm end plate.
   - On the humanoid, J1 turns to a lateral axis. J1 becomes shoulder pitch, J2 shoulder roll, J3 upper-arm yaw and J4 the elbow. That is quad00 `upper_layout()`'s pitch / roll / yaw / elbow chain.
2. **The arm is now 300 / 200 mm** (v9 was 260 / 220). That is closer to the humanoid's 318 / 410.
3. **The turret is shorter.** The J2 axis now sits 126 mm off the J1 back face, or 73 mm off the J1 output face.
   - The 60–90 mm asked for can't be done from the back face. The RS04 lobe (r 66) has to clear the turret disc on top of a 52 mm motor, so 126 mm is the minimum. Measured from the output face, it is in range.
   - **Desk:** a fixed 68 mm pedestal sits under the foot (four tabbed 1/8" plates plus a base plate). It puts J2 at 200 mm over the table.
   - **Body:** the same tube at 36 mm is the chest adapter. It bolts to the torso side plate's outer face (|y| = 87). That puts the J1 back face at |y| = 129.7 and J2 at |y| = 255.3, exactly on the humanoid's shoulder-roll axis.
4. **Checks pass.**
   - `fork_checks`, `audit` and `dfm` all pass: no interference across 207 poses and 0 DFM issues.
   - Every plate is 1/8" 5052 (the foot is 1/4", as in v9), and everything fits the 381 mm sheet.
   - `ROSE_ARM=v10 python3 arm/fork/fab.py` writes `fab-v10/`. `ROSE_ARM_PEDESTAL=0` gives the body build.
   - Two fixes were needed: the short turret's tower window web was 1.0 mm, and the bridge's centre hole web was 0.1 mm.
5. **Skin (render only).** `skin_v10.py` ports quad00 `skin.py`'s approach:
   - Lofted superellipse shells per segment with a 2.5 mm standoff.
   - Graphite pucks (0.78 x motor radius) on the J2 / J4 / J6 axes, gunmetal parting rings at J1 and J3, and the orange accent ring at J7.
   - The humanoid's palette: satin-black covers, graphite, gunmetal, orange, aluminium.
   - The plates stay the structure. The shells are not in any check.

## Risks

- **J3 is the weak joint.** With the upper arm level and the forearm swung out level sideways, J3 carries the elbow's whole load: 14.8 N·m at 2.5 kg. The RS02 is 17 N·m peak and about 4.4 N·m continuous (an estimate). So J3 holds only 0.15 kg continuously in that pose, and nothing with gripper v2.
  - On the desk, keep the forearm in the J2 plane (J1 does the azimuth) and J3 is unloaded.
  - On the humanoid, any "tray held out sideways" pose loads it.
  - Fixes that change the motors or cost: an RS04 at J3, a spring, or a brake.
- **J3 bearing tilt is not modelled.** RobStride gives no moment stiffness, so the sag figure assumes a rigid J3. The proximal upper arm is two open plates, and that is included.
- **J2 down-range drops to +20°.** The short turret costs this. On the humanoid it is shoulder roll (adduction toward the body); check it against the torso when the arm work goes there.
- **The body mount needs a 36 mm adapter.** J1 stands about 36 mm proud of the 146 mm chest shell under the shoulder skin. The chest cover would need a socket cut; that isn't done here.
- **The gripper v2 model is my estimate.** I lumped 485 g at 50 mm past the J7 output, with jaw tips 55 mm past the model's. On v9 that gives J2 1.19 kg, against the 1.27 kg in DESIGN-REVIEW.md, which looks like a different assumption.
- **The skin is visual only.** Shells overlap at the joints by design, like quad00's, and there is no clash check for them.
- **Unchanged from v9:** RS02 and RS00 thread depths are still marked "assumed" in the BOM.
