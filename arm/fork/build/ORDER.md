## 3. Notes

- **Gating item: RS04 x3.** Robotics Center shows BACKORDER, 4-6 weeks (RS02 and RS00 same there). OpenELAB US shows RS02/RS00 sold out, preorder, 10-20 working days (2-4 weeks, shipped from China or via US warehouse). aifitlab RS04 is backordered with no date. The openelab.io RS04 is preorder with no timeline; no openelab.com RS04 page found. Realistic: first motor in ~4-6 weeks, the full set ~6 weeks. Nothing else gates the build: aluminum, screws, bushings are 1-3 days from US stock.
- **Real stock claims differ.** A search snippet said Robotics Center RS-04 was 'in stock, ~48 h, San Francisco' at $255; the live page today says backorder at $295. Trust the page, confirm by email/quote before paying.
- **Amazon / AliExpress / Seeed / Foxtech: could not confirm** any official RobStride listing on those (search found none; Seeed only sells RS00/RS06 inside the reBot Arm B601-RS kit). The RobStride site (robstride.com) fetch returned no product data. Treat all above as third-party resellers.
- **Prices in the spec PDF** (README, 2026-07-13) are CNY ex-factory: RS00 CNY598, RS02 CNY699, RS04 CNY1199, RS05 CNY499 (about $85/$98/$168/$70). Resellers charge ~1.8-3x; ordering direct from RobStride in China could save money but adds freight, brokerage and tariff risk and slower lead.
- **US tariff risk: unconfirmed.** I could not verify the current duty rate on China-origin actuators in 2026. OpenELAB prices say 'duties and customs fees included' (DDP); Robotics Center says only 'tax calculated at checkout'; aifitlab ships from China, duty responsibility not stated. Budget 0-30% extra on non-DDP sellers.
- **EL05:** OpenELAB's closest product is the 'EduLite 05' (6 N.m peak, 1.8 N.m rated, $81.99). Confirm it equals the EL05 in the EL PDF (2026.09.22) before ordering.
- **48 V supply sizing.** BOM says LRS-350-48 (7.3 A, 350 W). RS04 is listed at 700 W output peak each; 3 RS04 plus 2 RS02 can exceed 350 W on a fast loaded move, and the supply hiccups on overcurrent. Recommend LRS-600-48 (12.5 A) for headroom, or keep the 350 W and limit current in firmware. Actuator regen cannot be absorbed by the LRS: add a bus TVS/brake-resistor or cap, and keep the supply fused.
- **E-stop on 48 V DC:** a 22 mm button's contacts are typically rated 3 A at 24 V DC, so do not break motor current through it directly. Use it to drop a DC-rated contactor or the PSU remote-on/off. A contactor (~$30) is not in the totals.
- **igus GFM-1618-03 / GFM-1012-03** were not found at distributors. Siblings in the family are $1-1.2 at TME US. The bushing length matters for the plate stack; verify the -03 variant on igus.com or use the nearest length only after checking the fit.
- **Sheet stock:** 3x 15x15 in is covered by one 24x48 in 1/8 in sheet (nest in nest/). The foot is 6.3 mm, so 1/4 in 12x12 works (9 in square needed).
- **Screw counts** above are BOM order list + spares: M3x10 8, M3x6 6, M4x10 44, M4x8 12, M5x12 18, M5x8 10, M6 6. Verify RS02 thread depth (marked 'assumed' in BOM) before cutting final lengths.
