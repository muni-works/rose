## 1. Soft limits: `soft_limits.py`, `pose_ok(q, payload, mount)`, table `soft_limits_v10.npz`

A pose passes when all of these hold:
- each joint is inside its skinned range (J2 -180..+10, J4 ±115, J6 ±105, rolls ±180);
- skin point clouds on non-adjacent links stay more than 6 mm apart (5 mm sampling + 1 mm), which catches the combined folds;
- desk: skin ≥ 5 mm above the table, and the jaws don't go through it;
- body: arm skin and tool ≥ 5 mm from the humanoid shell;
- the J3 cap holds.

`pose_ok` needs only numpy and the rose_fork FK.

Proof, with every accepted pose re-checked against the real meshes (skin x skin > 1 mm³) and the bare structure (`audit.interferes`):

| desk, random inside the structure ranges | 500 | 298 (60 %) | 0 |
| body_l, random | 300 | 183 (61 %) | 0 |

- Most rejections are the skinned per-joint ranges.
- The desk sweep ran before the "jaws through the table" check was added, so the accepted set has only shrunk since.

## 2. Thermal: `thermal_v10.py` (lumped network; every assumption is in its docstring)

Inputs from the RS spec book:
- RS04: 40 N·m on a 345 mm plate, 27 A pk, 0.16 Ω
- RS02: 7 N·m on a 150 mm plate, 7 A pk, 0.55 Ω
- RS00: 5 N·m on a 150 mm plate, 4.7 A pk, 1.5 Ω

Rated copper loss is 87 / 20 / 25 W. The winding limit is taken as 120 °C at 25 °C ambient.

| joint | motor | derating, closed skin | derating, v10.3 openings |
|---|---|---|---|
| J2 | RS04 | 33 % | **5 %**: open motor faces in a ring puck + Al flank panels |
| J3 | RS02 | 33 % | 20 %: dot-grid band over the can |
| J4 | RS04 | 34 % | 5 % |
| J6 | RS02 | 29 % | 5 % |
| J7 | RS00 | 42 % | 25 %: dot-grid band |

- **J2 hold with model jaws:** bare 1.43 kg, closed skin 0.31 kg, with the v10.3 openings **1.27 kg**.
- **The vented cap Cam specified fails:** J2/J4 hub caps with Ø1.3 holes on 2.3 mm hex give only 1.04 kg (`LYMB_PHI_CAP=0.5`). So the J2/J4/J6 faces are open behind a gunmetal bezel, and the upper-arm flank panels are warm-black anodized 1 mm aluminium on a gap-pad to the plates.
- **J4 / J6 hold, vented:** 4.53 / 1.35 kg.
- **J3:** continuous falls to 3.50 N·m, so the pose cap drops to 3.43 N·m (`pose_cap.MARGIN` 0.78).
- **The dot-grid is not modelled as holes:** it is drawn as a matte dead-front band at J3 and J7.
- **Open:** `fork_checks` doesn't apply the thermal derating yet; its holds are bare.
