## J2 margin back to ≥ 1.35 kg (distal mass first)

1. **Wrist 18 mm shorter.** The wrist end plate (J7's mount) moves from J6 lobe + 28 mm to lobe + 10 mm. That costs 18 mm of reach, and it is the biggest lever: J7, the tool and the payload all move closer to J2.
   - The J7 service loop still fits (2.3 mm to the can over ±180°).
   - The wrist's structural range opens to ±135°; its skinned range is ±100°.
2. **Distal plate windows.** The fore, yoke and wrist plates keep 9 mm flanges over their windows instead of 14 mm (`FLANGE_D`; webs ≥ 1.5 t, DFM clean). Sag was recomputed with the deeper windows.
3. **Skin wall.** The arm sleeves and the J4 / J6 rings go from 1.2 to 0.9 mm MJF PA12 with internal ribs. The turret, J2 ring, desk puck and J1 drum stay at 1.2 mm.
4. **Panel windows.** Each Al flank panel is windowed over its holder plate's own windows (40 % out). The heat path is unchanged, since the plate isn't there either.

## Shoulder read and ring taper (Cam, rose)

- **Ring taper down the arm:** J2 Ø137, J4 Ø126 (-8 %), J6 Ø96. Bezels are 3 / 2 / 1.5 mm.
  - The elbow lobe is now RS04 can + 2 mm (it was + 6; back-pattern web 6.75 mm, DFM clean), so the J4 ring can shrink.
  - Rose asked for 10-15 % smaller; the RS04 can (Ø120) limits it to -8 %.
- **Deltoid hood (humanoid only).** The turret shell carries on over the top third of the J2 ring, 0.5-3 mm proud, so the shoulder grows out of rose's crown line.
  - It covers a third of J2's dot-grid: J2's derate goes 1 % → 3 %, and J2's hold on the humanoid is 1.304 kg (still ≥ 1.27).
  - It stops the J2 backswing (abduction) at -75° on the humanoid.
  - The desk arm has no hood: J2 keeps -140 there and holds 1.374 kg.
- **Head.** cortex-full.loon (rose's full head, main @ 2cfecc3), pitched 35° down at rest about the head-pitch axis.

## Shoulder line on rose's torso pass 3

- **Turret shell.** It is now one asymmetric loft:
  - its top (arm -x: up on the humanoid at rest) runs on from rose's crown fillet: z 511.3 at the flank (|y| 186.6), 508.3 at |y| 190, then about 504 by |y| 202 (≈ 30°);
  - from there it runs long and flat at ≈ 503.5 into the J2 puck (top z 502.8);
  - its bottom narrows to the keyhole tower's neck under the seam circle.
  - It costs J2 40° of skinned range at the far end (-180 → -140, the arm swung back past vertical).
- **Chest adapter and desk tube.** Both now have open corners: each of the four walls is 110 wide on the 130 square. Nothing reaches past **r 85.1** about J1, inside rose's r 88.
  - The foot and base plate close the box.
  - **The foot shrank from Ø180 to Ø170 (r 85, bolts on r 72).** At r 90 it was the one thing inside rose's corner past its r 88 clearance; DFM is still clean.
  - Bending stiffness of the 41 mm tube drops 22 %, which is 0.004 mm at the jaws.
  - DFM is clean.
- **Rose's old fan box** (d1d9f69; gone in pass 3), x -83..-73, |y| 112..152, z 414..454, **overlapped our old foot** (Ø180, r 90 about J1 at |y| 128.1..134.4): the overlap was r 73..90, the box's inboard edge against the foot's rim. The Ø170 foot would still overlap it at r 73..85. It missed the adapter walls (x ±65) and the J1 can (r 60).
