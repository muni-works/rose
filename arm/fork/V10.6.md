# lymb v10.6: range wins; the skin is frozen

These are Cam's calls on v10.5, with rose's sign-off: flatten the biceps for range, drop the cap overlap, freeze the skin. Structure, structural ranges and DFM are unchanged from v10.5, and v9 is bit-identical.

## What limited range, and what changed

The v10.5 range loss did **not** come from the biceps' fore-aft depth: J2's axis runs fore-aft, so that depth never sweeps anything. The clash sweeps found three limiters:

| limiter | fix | skinned range, v10.5 → v10.6 |
|---|---|---|
| J2 backswing: the upper sleeve's top swept the turret's top band, which hugged the J2 ring just outside the seam circle | the turret top drops inside the seam circle above zs - 35. Desk: the turret is only the disc's skirt and the tower neck. Humanoid: rose's shoulder line holds for the first 15 mm off the flank, then drops to the neck. | desk J2 -95 → **-145** (+15); humanoid -75 → **-130** (+15) |
| humanoid J2: the deltoid hood over the J2 ring's top third (cost -25..-75 with the fuller sleeve) | **hood dropped** (Cam: no cap overlap; rose: keep J2's grid open). The ring is the hand-off. | (included above) |
| J4 fold: the upper sleeve's lateral width at the elbow (1.15-1.3 x 0.72 of full) | below J3, the side profile (fore-aft, what the side view shows) keeps rose's 0.75 → 0.72; the lateral width is the structure's own (≥ 0.8 x the depth) | J4 ±100 → **±110** |

The **biceps section is 1.3 : 1** (lateral : fore-aft) from the deltoid to J3. Its side profile is unchanged: fullest 27 % down, 0.75 at J3, 0.72 at the elbow. The inner (torso-side) face is held to the structure's own need at each station plus 2 mm, and the fullness goes outboard.

Known compromise: just below J3 the lateral width steps in, from 1.3 x 0.75 to about 0.8 x 0.75 of the full depth, over ~25 mm. The J3 hairline sits right at the top of that taper. It is visible from the front, not from the side.

The J2-ring-to-sleeve seam stays at 1 mm: it is the coaxial seam radius, and the J2 range above depends on it. Rose's optional 0.5 mm shadow gap would need the ring 0.5 mm bigger, so I didn't take it.

## Print STLs: `arm/fork/skin-stl/`

There are 28 STLs and a parts list, `PARTS.md` (file, part, link, finish, process, wall, qty, volume, bbox).
- **Desk arm:** 23 parts, about 520 cm³ of material.
- **Humanoid:** `*_body` (turret halves and the J1 face ring) replace `desk-puck`, `drum-J1` and the desk turret halves. The upper sleeve is handed: `upper-*` on the left arm, `upper-*_R` on the right, because J3's grid leaves the front face clean.
- **Bezels:** 6 (`cap-J2±1`, `cap-J4±1`, `cap-J6±1`).
- **The STLs are outer surfaces as solids.** Shell them to the listed wall (0.9 / 1.2 mm), inner face open.
- **The ring pucks' circumferential dot-grid is specified in PARTS.md, not meshed,** to keep the files small (4.7 MB for the set). The J3 and J7 grids are meshed.
