# lymb v10.7: the skin, print-ready

Cam approved the v10.6 skin for the order list. This pass makes the parts printable as-is. One geometry change rides along: the lateral step under J3 is now a taper.

## Print-ready parts: `arm/fork/skin-stl/print/` (+ `PRINT.md`; `skin-stl/PARTS.md` is a copy)

- **Source.** `skin_print.py` builds every part from the same lofts as the render skin (`skin_v10.LOFTS`), so they are the frozen v10.6 shapes. `skin_print_all.py` runs all three builds (desk, right-hand upper sleeve, humanoid) and writes the table.
- **Shells.** Outer loft minus an inner loft with every station inset by the wall (0.9 mm on the sleeves and the J4 / J6 rings, 1.2 mm elsewhere). The inner loft runs 3 mm past both ends, so each sleeve is open where the plates pass into the joints. The shells are cut back by the coaxial joint seams and split front / back on the parting plane.
- **Features.**
  - **Sleeve halves:** C-clips over the holder plates' straight edges (3.375 mm slot, 5 mm deep, two per plate edge). Magnet bosses on the parting plane: Ø6.2 x 3.2 pockets for N52 Ø6 x 3 discs, 2 per half. Features sit 0.05 mm inside the skin face, so there are no coplanar unions.
  - **Ring pucks:** the circumferential dot-grid, Ø1.3 on a 2.3 mm hex, meshed through the wall. Hole counts: J2 10 846, J4 7 224, J6 5 070. A 3-spoke spider on each face lands on 3 of the motor's own screws, where the Al standoffs go: output pattern on the A face, back pattern on the B face. The rings are 3MF (zip) because of the hole count: 2.3-4.8 MB each.
  - **J3 / J7 dot-grid fields:** through-holes.
  - **Bezels:** 0.9 mm annuli, bonded to the ring face. **J3 hairline / J7 accent:** strips of the sleeve's own section.
- **Checks.** All 28 files are watertight and manifold (every edge shared by exactly 2 faces). The set is 16 MB.
- **Cost.** The desk arm is 23 parts and 329 cm³ of MJF PA12: **~$132 at $0.40 / cm³**. That is a typical MJF service rate, **not a quote**: JLC3DP and Xometry both need an account for an instant MJF price. Dye / vapour-smoothing, the 6 turned bezels and the 2 SLA trims are extra. Per-part volumes and estimates are in PRINT.md.
- **Retention where no straight plate edge is inside the part:**
  - wrist halves: 2 seam magnet pairs plus the J7 accent ring over the joint line;
  - turret halves: magnets, located on the turret disc rim;
  - J1 drum: sits on the foot;
  - desk puck: captured under the pedestal base plate.

## The J3 taper (rose / Cam, non-blocking)

- **The change.** The lateral width under the J3 hairline now steps in over ~38 mm, blended across the station spacing (≈ 50 mm effective), instead of 25. The section at the hairline itself is unchanged, so the sections still match across J3.
- **Range, unchanged:** desk J2 -145..+15, J4 ±110, J3 ±180.
- **Coverage, unchanged** (table rebuilt): desk random 43 %, humanoid 41 %, 0 accepted poses clash.
- **Thermal and hold, unchanged:** J3 21 % / 3.47 N·m (cap 3.43); J2 1.370 kg; mass 8.56 kg; sag 0.45 mm.

## Notes

- 0.9 mm walls are at the low end for MJF PA12 (0.8-1.0 mm). The sleeves rely on their curvature plus the clip and boss features for stiffness. If the print service flags them, 1.0 mm adds ~10 g of moving mass and costs J2 ~0.01 kg.
- The J2 seam stays at 1 mm.
