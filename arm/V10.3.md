# lymb v10.3: soft limits, thermal, turret

v10.3 makes the v10.2 skin something the controller and the motors can live with:

- a combined-pose soft limit the controller can call;
- a thermal design that keeps J2 at ≥ 1.27 kg continuous with the skin on;
- no plate flank showing anywhere.

v9 is bit-identical (audit output and the `fab/` package match). Design only; nothing is ordered.

## 1. Turret: no plate flank shows

- **Keyhole tower plates.** Each turret tower plate is now the lobe disc on a straight 68 mm neck (`TOWER_NECK` 34 mm half-width), with tabs at ±22 mm and a 28 x 20 mm window.
  - The lobe sits inside the J2 puck. The neck sits inside a neck shell that rises from the turret shell into the J2 puck, cut by the same coaxial seam.
- **Skinned scenes.** The plates show only inside the open ring pucks, where they are black-anodized. The harness is drawn in black silicone, not orange.
- **Checked.** The v10.3 renders show no aluminium edge in any view.
- **Stiffness cost.** The neck's in-plane bending under the J2 moment is now in the sag figure: +0.15 mm, to 0.46 mm.

## 2. Combined-pose soft limits: `soft_limits.py`

`pose_ok(q, payload, mount)` returns True when the pose is safe to command; `why(...)` returns the first reason it isn't. `mount` is `'desk'`, `'body_l'` or `'body_r'`, and `q` is in radians. It needs numpy and the rose_fork FK. The geometry table is `soft_limits_v10.npz`: about 62k skin surface points at 5 mm spacing, plus the humanoid's shell at 6 mm voxels.

A pose passes only if all of these hold:

1. every joint is inside its skinned range (J2 -180..+10, J4 ±115, J6 ±105, J1 / J3 / J7 ±180);
2. no two skin parts on links that move relative to each other, and are not joined directly by one joint, come within 6 mm (5 mm sampling + 1 mm). This is the combined-fold rule: J4 + J6 folding the wrist into the upper arm, J2 + J4 bringing the wrist into the desk puck. Directly joined pairs are safe by construction: the coaxial 1 mm seam;
3. desk mount: all skin ≥ 5 mm above the table, and the jaws may touch it but not go through it. Body mount: arm skin and tool ≥ 5 mm from the humanoid's shell (the shoulder itself sits in the torso corner and is exempt);
4. the J3 static torque is under the cap (`pose_cap.j3_ok`) at that payload.

**Proof.** Every pose `pose_ok` accepts was re-checked against the real meshes (skin x skin overlap > 1 mm³) and the bare structure (`audit.interferes`):

| sweep | poses | kept | kept but clashing |
|---|---|---|---|
| desk, random inside the structure's ranges (with the jaws-through-desk check) | 500 | 291 (**58 %**) | **0** |
| humanoid left arm, random inside the structure's ranges | 300 | 183 (**61 %**) | **0** |
| desk, fold grid J2 x J4 x J6 over the skinned ranges, 15° steps | 3120 | 2674 (86 %) | **0** |

Rejections: on the desk random sweep, per-joint range 180, desk clearance 24, J3 cap 3, fold 2; on the fold grid, desk 381, fold 65. The body sweep is against rose's torso pass 1 (claude/quad00-torso-v103 @ a2c2207): ≥ 5 mm skin to torso.

The fraction of the structure's range kept is 58-61 % (86 % of the skinned fold grid). Most of the loss is the skin's own single-joint stops at J4 (±115 against -150..130) and J6 (±105 against ±130).

## 3. Thermal: `thermal_v10.py` (lumped network; every assumption is in its docstring)

Inputs from the RS spec book (~/Developer/robstride_assets, 2026-10-09):

| motor | rated | sink plate | phase current | line R | rated copper loss |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| RS04 | 40 N·m | 345 mm Al | 27 A pk | 0.16 Ω | 87 W |
| RS02 | 7 N·m | 150 mm Al | 7 A pk | 0.55 Ω | 20 W |
| RS00 | 5 N·m | 150 mm Al | 4.7 A pk | 1.5 Ω | 25 W |

- The winding limit is 120 °C in 25 °C air.
- Each motor's winding-to-mount resistance is backed out of its manual's sink plate (h = 10 W/m²K, fin efficiency 0.7).
- In the arm, the motor sheds heat through its can and the plates bolted to it (plate efficiency 0.5), through the enclosed air (h_in = 5 W/m²K), and through the shell.
- Continuous torque scales as √(allowed loss).

| joint | motor | closed skin | **v10.3 design of record** |
|---|---|---|---|
| J2 | RS04 | -27 % | **-1 %** |
| J3 | RS02 | -32 % | -19 % |
| J4 | RS04 | -28 % | -1 % |
| J6 | RS02 | -25 % | -6 % |
| J7 | RS00 | -39 % | -23 % |

**The v10.3 design of record (Cam picked it on 2026-10-21):**

- **J2, J4, J6 pucks are open graphite rings.**
  - The motor's own black face is open to air behind a 3 mm gunmetal bezel.
  - The 8 mm annulus round the can is open at both ends.
  - The ring's circumference carries the dot-grid (Ø1.3 holes on a 2.3 mm hex, 29 % open), so the can's barrel sees air.
- **Aluminium flank panels on the upper arm.** Its flat front and back faces over the J2 and J4 holder plates are 0.6 mm 5052 panels, warm-black anodized, on a 0.25 mm gap pad (~3 W/mK) to the plates. The plates keep their own convection area (+0.20 K/W through pad and panel).
- **Dot-grid over the roll cans.** J3 and J7 get the dot-grid on the four flat faces of the sleeve, over the can (modelled as real holes, 2.5 mm deep).

Assumptions to check on the elbow rig's heat test:
- h = 10 W/m²K everywhere outside;
- h_in = 5 W/m²K in the enclosed air;
- dot-grid effectiveness 0.5 of an open face;
- the open annulus counts as a fully exposed barrel;
- the 0.20 K/W pad + panel path.

### J1 on the humanoid, in rose's torso corner (`thermal_v10.j1_body`)

J1's back face conducts through the 1/4" foot, the 41 mm chest adapter (4 x 1/8" 5052) and the 6.35 mm torso side plate plus half its decks, to enclosed chest air and this side's half of the chest shell. The corner itself is enclosed air. Holding 23.5 N·m (rose's arms-forward at 1.27 kg, bare arm) dissipates 30 W; 24.7 N·m with the skin's mass dissipates 33 W.

| case | J1 continuous | arms-forward hold | time to 120 °C at 23.5 N·m | at 24.7 N·m |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| plate only | 23.9 N·m | 1.16 kg | never (steady) | ~119 min |
| **plate + open ring (v10.3 design)** | **26.9 N·m** | **1.56 kg** | never | never |
| plate + fan 1.3 L/s | 33.1 N·m | 2.41 kg | never | never |
| plate + open ring + fan | 34.7 N·m | 2.62 kg | never | never |

**No fan.** With the open ring face, J1 holds the rated load indefinitely, and J2 (1.27 kg) stays the arm's limit.
- The times come from a one-node transient (850 J/K), so they are upper bounds.
- The full interface for rose is in `TORSO-INTERFACE.md`: J1 drum numbers, the J2 swept envelope (`interface/j1j2_envelope_l.stl`), puck specs, heat per motor, and deltoid-cap ownership.
- **Placement change:** the arm's J1 now sits on quad00's shoulder-pitch axis (x 0, z 434.3), not the shoulder-roll height. This matches rose's torso; J2 is at |y| 260.1, z 434.3.

## Still open

- **J2 is exactly at budget (1.271 kg).** Every gram outboard costs about 0.4 g of hold. The skin fastening (clips, standoffs) isn't modelled, so it will push J2 under 1.27 unless something else gets lighter.
- **The thermal model is a lumped estimate.** It needs the elbow rig's heat test, especially the dot-grid and open-annulus assumptions.
- **Skin fastening.** No new plate holes are allowed, so it has to be clips onto existing features. Not designed yet.
- **The soft-limit table is a point cloud.** If the skin changes, rebuild it with `soft_limits.py build <hum dir>` and re-run the sweeps.
- **Humanoid torso.** Rose's sloped top and deltoid cap belong to the body session (quad00).
