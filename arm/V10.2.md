# lymb v10.2: Rose's language

This pass applies Rose's v10.1 review and the coordinator's v10.2 brief. Rose's review wins where the two differ. The skin is still a render-only layer, but four structure changes came with it.

## Structure

The checks were re-run after these changes.

- **Keyhole plates.** Every v10 fork plate (`dplate`) now has a full lobe disc on a straight neck, instead of tangent flanks. The plates taper toward each joint instead of flaring, so the sleeves can taper too.
  - The upperd holder plates went from a half-height of 50 to 40 mm, and their covers from 40 to 30 mm.
- **Foot.** r 90 mm, bolts on a 76 mm radius.
- **Desk tube.** 130 mm square, 45 mm tall. J2 now sits at 177 mm over the table, down from 200.
- **Pedestal base plate.** Round, r 94 mm.

## Skin (`skin_v10.skin_parts`)

- **Sleeves, not hulls.** Each link is one loft of rounded-rectangle stations, corner 0.30 x the half-depth (cortex plan corner). The stations sit on a straight taper and are raised only where the structure needs it.
  - The upper arm is widest at the deltoid and about 0.7x at the elbow. The forearm is slimmer than the upper arm.
- **Coaxial seams.** At every pitch joint, both sleeves are cut back by one cylinder coaxial with the joint, r = puck + 1 mm.
  - The 1 mm parting gap is invariant under rotation, so no sliver or pinch can appear anywhere in the joint's range. This fixes the elbow slivers.
- **Pucks.** Flush graphite pucks as wide as the sleeves, with a flush gunmetal hub cap. The structure's printed hub caps are left out of skinned renders.
- **J3.** The upper arm is one loft, split by a 1.5 mm gap at J3 with a flush gunmetal ring in the gap. The sleeve covers J3's bolt circle.
- **Cable.** It runs inside the sleeves, with service loops at J3 and J7.
- **Desk base.** One calm cylinder, the J1 drum + 8 mm, with a 14 mm top round. The arm grows out of a puck; the skirt is gone.
- **Humanoid shoulder.** J1 lives inside the torso's top corner.
  - The chest adapter (41 mm) puts J1's output face flush on the cover's flank at |y| = 186.6. Only a flush graphite disc and gunmetal ring show.
  - That lands J2 at |y| = 260 (the humanoid's own shoulder roll is at 255).
  - The turret shell runs from the flank to the J2 puck, and the upper sleeve hangs from it.
  - The v10.1 chest recess is off (`CHEST_SOCKET` unset).
- **Palette.** Warm black #1e1813 satin, entered as linear 0.016 / 0.012 / 0.009 because the renderer is linear; the humanoid's covers take the same tone. Joints are graphite and gunmetal. Make green #39ff14 is the only neon: a flush ring in the wrist sleeve, replacing the orange.
- **Head.** The cortex (`head/cortex.loon`) on the full body's neck dock, Make colorway, at 1:1. The old cube head is dropped.
- **Render fix.** vcad composes `[rotate]` differently for imported meshes than for sketches. The shells are therefore posed in Python and imported untransformed, named by content hash because vcad caches meshes by path. v10 and v10.1 renders had the shells mis-rotated on compound poses.

## Harness and skin checks

- **Roll loops (exterior note: a straight chord cuts the motors at large roll).** The J3 and J7 loops wrap their cartridge on the stator side and hand over to the rotor side only past the bridge (J3) or the tool flange (J7). The chord that swings through the axis at large roll therefore meets no motor.
  - Check: `fork_checks.cable_roll_clearance()`, ±180° in 10° steps, cable surface to motor can.
  - Result: **J3 1.9 mm, J7 2.3 mm** minimum.
  - The chord at J3 can still brush the bridge plate's corners at large roll. Only the motors are checked.
- **Skin clash, `skin_clash.py`.** It follows the exterior clash.py rule: each pair is swept over only the joints between them, at the structure's ranges, and a clash is more than 1 mm³ of shared volume. It is my own check over these meshes, not their clash.py: theirs reads `exterior.covers()` and v9 frames.
  - Ranges with the skin on, each joint alone: J2 -180..+10 (structure +25), J4 ±115 (structure -150..+130), J6 ±105 (structure ±130), rolls ±180.
  - The skin now sets the J4 / J6 / J2 stops. This brings the soft limits back to v9's scenario limits for J4 (±115); J6 drops from ±120 to ±105.
  - **Still clashing** at combined folds: J4 -150 with J6 -130 folds the wrist into the upper arm; J2 -180 with J4 -150 brings the wrist into the desk puck. Those poses are outside the skinned single-joint ranges anyway. A combined-pose soft limit is still open.
- **Exterior carry-over rules.**
  - Only the straddler wears a raised shell: the pucks belong to turret, fore and wrist, and the straddled link's sleeve is cut back by the same coaxial seam.
  - Round, rotation-invariant hubs only; no cheeks.
  - The turret shell now stops where the J2 seam circle starts, so the upper sleeve swings past it.
- **CMF.** Warm black #1e1813 PC/ABS, 2 mm wall, 2.5 mm standoff. No new plate holes: the skin is meant to clip to existing features, but no fastening is designed yet.
- **Heat.** The skin encloses J2, J3, J4, J6 and J7 (J1 sits in the body corner on the humanoid). The hold margin is heat-limited at about 13 %. In the design, the J2 and J4 puck faces (the gunmetal hub-cap discs) are perforated vents: Ø1.3 mm holes on a 2.3 mm hex grid, the one dot-grid in the arm. They are rendered solid, and no thermal model was run.

## Still open

- **Turret plate edges still show on the desk.** Since the turret shell was shortened to clear the J2 swing, the tower plates' flanks show between the turret shell and the J2 puck (`desk-34`). The tower plates need the keyhole treatment, or the J2 puck must grow to cover them.

- **The humanoid's torso is quad00's.** Angling its top front and back, and growing the corners over the turret, belongs in quad00 (Rose's session). Here J1 is flush in the existing corner, but the turret shell between the flank and J2 still reads as a horizontal block.
- **No deltoid cap yet.** A cap continuing the torso slope over J2 would need the torso change first.
- **Skin vs torso is unchecked** on the humanoid, and so are combined-pose soft limits (see above). J1 = ±90 on the body was only rendered.
- **The cable shows briefly** at the wrist-to-tool crossing.
- **The head choice is mine.** The cortex is rendered 1:1 (the product's own size), not scaled to the body.
- **Not implemented:** dot-grids and dead-front windows; no arm feature needs air or light yet.
- **Not used:** vcad `[skin]` (the full/skin.py rolling-ball method) needs an unmerged vcad branch, so the sleeves are explicit lofts.
