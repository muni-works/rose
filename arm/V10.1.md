# lymb v10.1: elegance passes and the J3 pose cap

Same structure as v10 (V10.md). Only the pedestal base plate changed: it is now round, r 122, so it sits under the skirt. The skin, the cable route and the renders were redone, and a pose cap was added.

## What changed

- **One vocabulary at every pitch joint.** Each pitch joint (J2, J4, J6) is now a graphite drum: coaxial, r = the joint lobe + 3.5 mm, as wide as the straddler, with gunmetal hub caps on both faces.
  - The drum swallows both links' lobes, so no plate edge can show at a joint. That fixes the edges poking through at the upper arm and turret in v10.
  - The shells run drum to drum, clipped at the joint axes, so their ends live inside the drums.
- **The upper arm is one loft** over upper + upperd, split at the J3 plane. The two halves share their section there, the parting line is continuous, and a gunmetal band marks it.
- **Mount and turret.**
  - The J1 drum is round. The desk pedestal is a flared round skirt on the same axis: a shoulder drum standing on the desk, echoing the humanoid's shoulder.
  - A graphite drum hides the foot, and gunmetal bands sit at the J1 output and the pedestal seam.
  - The turret shell runs from the J1 output to the J2 drum.
- **Chest socket on the humanoid.** In the render/export layer only (`humanoid_export.py`):
  - A recess of r 75 is cut through the chest cover round the J1 axis, with a gunmetal bezel on the shell surface.
  - J1's graphite drum runs in through it to the torso side plate, so the 36 mm protrusion now reads like the humanoid's own shoulder drum.
- **Cable.**
  - Pitch joints: through the sleeves, as in v9.
  - New 3/4-turn service loops round the J3 and J7 cartridges (`rose_fork.cable_points`, v10 only).
  - The skin lofts include the harness points, so the cable stays inside the shells.
- **Palette unchanged across desk and humanoid:** satin-black shells, graphite drums, gunmetal caps and bands, one orange ring at J7.

## J3 pose cap (RS02 kept at J3, poses capped in software)

`pose_cap.py`: `j3_torque(q, payload, R0)` is the model's static torque about J3 (every mass outboard of J3, plus the payload at the jaw tips). `j3_ok()` rejects poses above **3.52 N·m**, which is the RS02's estimated 4.4 N·m continuous rating x 0.8.

`build/measure/poses.py` now drops any gate pose that fails `cap_ok()` (v9 always passes).

Allowed envelope (`python3 arm/pose_cap.py`):

| payload | desk: upper arm level, elbow 90, J3 out of plane | humanoid: elbow 90 forward, forearm off straight-ahead, arm raised 0 / 45 / 90° | pose grid (J2 x J3 x J4) passing |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 kg | up to 71° | ±90 / ±90 / ±71° | 99 % |
| 0.5 kg | up to 36° | ±90 / ±57 / ±36° | 84 % |
| 1.0 kg | up to 25° | ±90 / ±37 / ±25° | 71 % |

What this means in use:
- **Desk:** keep the forearm in the J2 plane (J1 does the azimuth) and J3 is free. Out-of-plane reaches with a load are capped.
- **Humanoid:** with the upper arm hanging, J3 is unloaded. As the shoulder raises the arm, sideways forearm poses with a load get capped.
- **Never allowed continuously:** a sideways tray hold, 1 kg with the forearm level and swung out more than 25°.

## Still open

- **The RS02 4.4 N·m continuous rating is an estimate**, and the cap rests on it. The rana dyno should measure it.
- **The pose cap is only checked in the gate list.** The measurement `arm.py` IK is still v9-shaped (no J3), so the poses.py hook only bites once that IK learns v10. The cap is also not enforced in any runtime controller yet.
- **The skin is still render-only, with no clash check.** The drums are wider than the plates, so their swept clearance against neighbours is unchecked. The sibling `exterior/clash.py` is v9-only and uncommitted.
- **The chest recess exists only in the render layer.** quad00's cover-chest needs the real cut.
- **The upper arm loft is a convex hull,** so it carries the full lobe height along its whole length. It reads as a bone, not a taper. A non-hull loft would slim it.
